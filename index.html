Conflict Management Styles Assessment

Discover how you naturally respond to conflict — and what that means for your growth as a nursing leader.

A note from your instructor

As a nurse, you will encounter conflict — with colleagues, with patients, with systems that don't always work the way they should. This isn't a flaw in your career path; it's a reality of working in high-stakes, team-based care. The question isn't whether you'll face conflict, but how you'll navigate it when it arrives.


This assessment gives you a window into your own instincts. Understanding your default style — and knowing when it serves you and when it doesn't — is one of the most practical leadership tools you can carry into practice. Every nurse leads. This is part of learning how.

How to use your results
1

Recognize your go-to response under pressure — and honestly ask yourself whether it's actually serving the situation

2

Understand all five styles so you can flex into them intentionally — effective leaders aren't locked into one mode

3

Connect this to how your team functions — your conflict style shapes the environment and culture you create for others

Before you begin

This takes about 5 minutes. Read each statement and rate how often it describes you — from 1 (rarely) to 5 (always). Be honest; there are no right or wrong answers. Your results are calculated right in your browser and nothing is collected or stored. You're welcome to print or save your results, but this assessment is for you.
